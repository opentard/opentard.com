What Is OpenTard

A lightweight OpenClaw alternative that keeps things simple and your credentials safe.

Lightweight

Minimal footprint, runs anywhere. No bloated runtimes or heavy dependencies to wrestle with.

OpenRouter First

One API key, hundreds of models. Route to any LLM through OpenRouter without vendor lock-in.

Credential-Safe

Standalone auth proxies keep your secrets out of AI context. The agent never sees your tokens.

How It Works

Three simple layers keep your AI workflow fast and your credentials isolated.

1

You talk to OpenTard

Give the agent a task in natural language. It plans and executes autonomously.

2

Agent routes via OpenRouter

One API key accesses any LLM — GPT-4, Claude, Llama, Mistral, and more.

3

Auth proxies handle secrets

Service credentials live in isolated proxies. AI only gets results, never tokens.

Auth Proxy Architecture

The AI agent and your credentials never share context.
